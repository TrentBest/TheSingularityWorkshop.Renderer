01 · The model

Event Horizons organize work around observation.

An Event Horizon is a policy boundary: crossing it can change the representation and the work required to maintain it. Horizons are not fixed graphics-engine LOD rings; their meaning depends on observer context and experience policy.

Observer

What matters from here?

Position, intent, visibility, task, and semantic needs influence which information is relevant. Different observers can legitimately require different representations of the same world.

Observation model ↗
Representation

Identity outlives form.

A stable entity can have multiple materialized representations. Fidelity, cadence, semantic detail, visibility, and interaction availability are policy dimensions—not just polygon count.

Representation contracts ↗
Buckets

Invalidate only what changed.

Independent work buckets own their inputs, dependencies, revisions, and validity. A lighting change need not discard an unrelated shadow or geometry result.

Bucket model ↗
Composition

Prepare once; execute the plan.

MicroBundles contribute capabilities and defaults. Forge-time composition prepares bucket-local FSM pipelines; runtime executes those structures and responds to changing data without rerunning arbitration.
